IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before you book

What is included in hourly charter?

A private vehicle and driver for the confirmed duration. Your quotation should state waiting, parking, ERP, overtime and cross-border terms.

Are attraction tickets included?

No. Admission tickets are normally purchased separately unless your written quotation says otherwise.

Can the itinerary change during the trip?

Yes, within the booked hours and service area, subject to traffic, parking access and the driver's ability to complete the trip safely.

What happens if the booking exceeds the reserved hours?

Additional-hour charges apply according to the confirmed quotation and availability of the driver and vehicle.
